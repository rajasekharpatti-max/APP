Privacy Policy — Anusha Jewelry

Effective: 14 August 2026. App publisher: Anusha Jewelry, Nellore, Andhra Pradesh.

This app is a shop book for the store owner. It is not a customer-facing marketplace.

What is stored

Customer names, phone numbers, addresses, orders, khata (credit) amounts, old-gold lots, and gold rates. Data is stored only on the device (and in JSON backups you export yourself).

What is not done

  • No account login to our servers
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no sale of data
  • No upload of customer data to the internet unless you choose WhatsApp or export a file

WhatsApp

If you tap WhatsApp due, the phone number and message stay on your device and are opened in WhatsApp. That is under WhatsApp’s policy.

Backups

JSON backups you save to Downloads or Drive are your responsibility. Keep them private.

Contact

Anusha Jewelry, Nellore. Phone listed in the shop profile inside the app.
